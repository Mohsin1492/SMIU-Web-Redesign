About the university

About Sindh Madressatul Islam University

Chartered by the Government of Sindh and recognized by the Higher Education Commission of Pakistan, SMIU connects a 19th-century educational tradition with modern degree programs in Karachi.

Our history

Sindh Madressatul Islam University is among the oldest institutions in South Asia. It started as a school in 1885, was elevated to a college in 1943, and became a chartered university in February 2012.

The campus sits in Karachi’s commercial center, near I.I. Chundrigar Road (formerly McLeod Road), close to landmarks such as Habib Bank Plaza and MCB Tower. Spread over more than eight acres, the site includes colonial-era buildings designed by architect James Strachan in the 1880s.

SMIU is also developing an additional campus at Education City near Karachi, where land has been allotted for future expansion.

Connection with Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah

The Founder of Pakistan studied at this institution for about four and a half years, from 1887 to 1892. That legacy remains central to SMIU’s identity and its motto.

“Enter to learn — Go forth to Serve” — historic creed associated with Sindh Madressatul Islam University.

The university presents itself as a place that has educated leaders, educationists, and citizens who serve society—continuing the spirit of learning and public responsibility embodied by the Quaid.

Mission & vision

Vision

In accordance with its historic creed, SMIU strives to develop society through high-quality education and research while promoting national integration, inter-cultural harmony, and respect for diversity.

Mission

The mission is to create an excellent environment for education, research, and learning in order to produce leaders in various walks of life, and to build linkages with leading national and international institutions.

Core values (summary)

  • Strong relationships with students, faculty, alumni, and volunteers
  • Effective teaching methodology and leading-edge research
  • Development of analytical, interpersonal, and leadership skills
  • Freedom of thought and expression for independent, creative thinkers
  • Commitment to responsible citizenship and global leadership

Mission and vision wording summarized from official SMIU publications. See smiu.edu.pk/about for full text.

Educational significance today

As a public chartered university, SMIU offers diversified academic departments—from computer science and software engineering to business, media, education, and environmental sciences—supporting Karachi’s role as a regional center for higher learning.
