Campus life

Campus & Facilities

The main campus combines heritage architecture with modern academic infrastructure in central Karachi, plus planned expansion at Education City.

Campus overview

SMIU’s main campus is located in Karachi’s commercial hub, near I.I. Chundrigar Road, in the vicinity of Habib Bank Plaza and MCB Tower. The site covers more than eight acres and includes colonial-era buildings designed by James Strachan in the 1880s.

The university is also developing an additional campus at Education City near Karachi, with land allotted for future growth. Official contact materials list the address as Hasrat Mohani Road, Karachi-74000.

Historic architecture

Heritage structures contribute to the university’s identity as one of South Asia’s longstanding educational institutions.

Colonial-era buildings

Stone and brick architecture reflects 19th-century design, preserved as part of the living campus.

Campus landmarks

Religious and cultural spaces sit alongside academic buildings, supporting daily student life.

Learning spaces & facilities

The following facilities are described in SMIU’s student handbook and related official materials:

Library

Allama I.I. Kazi Library supports study and research. The library building occupies a historic structure on campus.

Computer & IT labs

Computing departments provide networked laboratories with modern hardware for programming, networking, and digital design courses.

Specialized labs & studios

Official resources mention electronic labs, video conferencing halls, TV/radio studio, and environmental laboratory work where programs require it.

Auditorium & conference rooms

Venues for seminars, convocations, and academic events.

Student amenities

Cafeteria, indoor sports hall, and gymnasium are listed among main-campus facilities in the student handbook.

IT tower & model school

University reports reference a constructed Information Technology Tower and SMIU Model School supporting expanded academic activity—verify access policies on official channels.
