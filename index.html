Admissions Academic Programs

A university with deep roots in Sindh

SMIU began as a school in 1885, became a college in 1943, and was chartered as a university in February 2012. The Founder of Pakistan, Quaid-e-Azam Mohammad Ali Jinnah, studied here for about four and a half years (1887–1892).

Today the university offers undergraduate, graduate, and postgraduate programs across business, computing, media, education, environmental sciences, and social development—serving students in the commercial hub of Karachi.

Campus highlights

The main campus spans more than eight acres near I.I. Chundrigar Road, with colonial-era buildings and modern learning facilities.

Allama I.I. Kazi Library

The library supports research and study in a historic campus setting. Additional learning spaces include computer labs, studios, and conference facilities.
