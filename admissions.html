Admissions

Undergraduate, graduate, and postgraduate admissions are managed through SMIU’s official online portal and admission office. Use this page as a guide—always confirm current cycles, fees, and criteria on official resources.

Before you apply

SMIU publishes program-specific minimum marks, seat allocation, fee schedules, and academic policies on its admissions section. Requirements vary by degree level (for example, intermediate qualifications for many BS programs, 16-year education for MS/MBA paths, and NTS GAT scores where applicable).

Important: This student website does not list current admission deadlines, application fees, or test dates. Visit smiu.edu.pk/admissions and admission.smiu.edu.pk for up-to-date information before applying.

General application process

The steps below summarize the procedure described on SMIU’s “How to Apply” page. Wording and order may change; follow live instructions on the portal.

  1. Create an online account at the admission portal. Enter your CNIC carefully—it typically cannot be changed later.
  2. Complete the online form with accurate academic history. Keep SSC, HSC, and degree documents ready (requirements depend on program level).
  3. Pay the application processing fee using the generated fee voucher at a designated bank branch, then upload the paid challan and required documents to the portal.
  4. Department review — applications are scrutinized against published criteria. Approved applicants may receive admit cards, prospectus, and sample materials via the portal where applicable.
  5. Testing & interviews — undergraduate applicants may be required to sit on-campus computer-based tests; interviews may be scheduled for shortlisted candidates. Graduate and postgraduate applicants often submit NTS results as described in official policy.
  6. Offer & enrollment — successful candidates receive offer letters and must submit attested documents and fees by the deadline stated in the offer.

Detailed document checklists for undergraduate, graduate, and postgraduate programs are on How to Apply — SMIU.
