Departments

SMIU organizes teaching and research through specialized departments spanning technology, business, social sciences, media, education, and environmental studies.

Schools & departments

Summaries below are based on official SMIU department listings and published reports. Follow each link for curricula, faculty, and contact details.

Department of Computer Science

Offers BS programs in Computer Science, Information Technology, and Cyber Security, plus MS and PhD in Computer Science. Emphasizes modern labs and industry proximity in Karachi’s IT hub.

Department of Software Engineering

Delivers BS Software Engineering and BS Data Science with specializations such as modeling & simulation, game systems, and intelligent systems—aligned with HEC curricula.

Artificial Intelligence & Mathematical Sciences

Supports undergraduate AI and mathematics-related programs and postgraduate study including PhD Artificial Intelligence and PhD Mathematics per admissions listings.

Department of Business Administration

Covers BBA and MBA pathways and PhD Management Sciences, focusing on management, entrepreneurship, and professional skills for the public and private sectors.
